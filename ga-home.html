HORIZONS Internal Marketing Tool

Horizons Ads Manager

Horizons Ads Manager is an internal marketing operations application operated by The Horizons (عز الآفاق) to manage and analyze authorized Google Ads accounts.

What Horizons Ads Manager does

The application helps authorized HORIZONS marketing staff review advertising performance, manage campaigns, ads, keywords, budgets, conversion settings, and produce reports for Google Ads accounts that have granted the company access.

Google account access

Google account access is used only after authorization and only for the advertising accounts and related Google services required to deliver campaign management and reporting services. Access is not sold or used for unrelated profiling.

Who can use it

This application is intended for HORIZONS staff and authorized business users managing accounts for which HORIZONS has permission. It is not offered as a public consumer product.
