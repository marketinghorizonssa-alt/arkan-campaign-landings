Privacy Policy

HORIZONS Google Ads Forecast Automation · The Horizons (عز الآفاق)

This policy explains how the application accesses and uses Google user data when an authorized user connects a Google account.

Data we may access

  • Google Ads account identifiers and account structure.
  • Campaigns, ad groups, ads, keywords, budgets, targeting and status information.
  • Advertising performance and reporting metrics.
  • Conversion-related configuration when required for campaign management.
  • Basic Google account information required to authenticate the authorized user.

How we use Google data

Google data is used only to provide advertising account management, reporting, analysis, forecasting, troubleshooting, and optimization services for accounts that HORIZONS is authorized to manage.

Storage and retention

Data may be temporarily stored in company-controlled systems, spreadsheets, logs, or application infrastructure where needed to provide the service, maintain audit history, troubleshoot errors, or prepare reports. Data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes or contractual obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell Google user data. We do not share Google user data with third parties except service providers acting on our behalf where necessary to operate the application, comply with law, or deliver authorized services.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect access credentials and data. Access is restricted to authorized personnel and systems.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revocation and deletion

Users can revoke Google access through their Google Account permissions. Requests to delete data associated with this application may be submitted using the process described on our Data Deletion & Revocation page.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact details published on thehorizons.sa or the support page.
