Data Controls

Data Deletion & Access Revocation

Horizons Ads Manager · The Horizons (عز الآفاق)

Revoke Google access

An authorized user may revoke Horizons Ads Manager's access to their Google account at any time from the Google Account third-party access/security settings.

Request deletion

To request deletion of application data associated with your Google account or advertising accounts, contact HORIZONS using the contact information on thehorizons.sa. Include enough information to identify the connected account and the data you want removed.

What happens next

After verifying the request and authority of the requester, HORIZONS will remove applicable stored application data unless retention is required by law, contractual obligations, security, fraud prevention, or legitimate audit requirements.

Disconnecting an advertising account

Removing HORIZONS or the application's authorized identity from Google Ads account access will prevent future API access to that account.
